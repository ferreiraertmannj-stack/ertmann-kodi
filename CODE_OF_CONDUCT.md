# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and professional collaboration
environment for everyone participating in the Ertmann Kodi Platform.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in technical discussions.
- Focus feedback on code, documentation, and observable behavior.
- Respect different experience levels, devices, and accessibility needs.
- Report security concerns privately rather than in public issues.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Sharing private or sensitive information without permission.
- Deliberately introducing malicious, incompatible, or unapproved code.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, or restrict
participation when these standards are not met. Report concerns through a
private maintainer channel made available by Ertmann Tech.
